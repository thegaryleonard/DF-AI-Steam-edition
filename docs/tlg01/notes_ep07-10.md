## FPS / traffic / don't-do-X

- Don't rely on building destroyers ignoring doors (Ep07 50:50).
- Don't leave up/down stairs open into caverns. Bypass them and floor over the gap (Ep07 96:41).
- Don't make long winding stair paths for hauling; dwarves can die of thirst on the way (Ep08 38:45).
- Cavern invasions can wreck FPS; consider turning them off (Ep07 92:00).
- Don't do huge outside digs before the interior is fortified (Ep09 8:02).
- Keep defensive levers inside the civilian burrow (Ep09 9:00).
- Animal pens shouldn't double as hallways or sit next to butchers (Ep10 16:23).
- Traders come too often; ignore them and keep the fort sealed (Ep10 95:13, Ep09 88:49).
- Restrict traffic in dangerous areas (traffic "restricted" painted over the swim trainer, Ep10 73:43). Children may still wander into the last place they worked.
- Don't buy backpacks; don't let a junk finished-goods pile grab artifacts (Ep07 49:49, 30:24).
