# Lore of The Enchanted Planes (what the Herald has learned so far)

THIS IS ONE WORLD'S EXAMPLE, used to brief library writers. The paper itself learns lore at runtime:
herald_lore.py watches play and records each fort in herald/lore.json per world (dates, peak population,
deaths, sieges, record stockpiles, runaway work orders, and a fate when the next fort starts, with occupiers
taken from the rumours). Library entries reach that through slots: {past_fort} {past_fort_fate} {past_fort_years}
{record} {record_fort} (condition has_past_fort) and {civ} {monarch} {liaison} {enemy} {enemy_race} (at_war).
Entries that name things from THIS world literally carry "world": "The Enchanted Planes" and print nowhere else.

Real history from the streamed df-ai game. Library writers: draw jokes from these facts. Rules:
- Names of PAST forts, world groups and world figures below are permanent world history, so you may use them literally.
- Do NOT write the names of current citizens literally (forts change); use {citizen}/{dwarf}/{captain}/... slots instead.
- The fortress is run by an AI overseer ("the Overseer", "the management", "the strategy") that makes famously odd decisions; the dwarves have opinions about it. Never call it an AI/computer/robot in-world: it is the Overseer.

## The fortress line (same world, one after another)
1. **Searabbeys** (the first; years ~100-105). No meat or fish for ages ("the Meat Watch"); 2,000+ drinks; 800+ loose stones; 20 farm plots; 13 dogs; a miner, Bomrek Enshalcatten, was "found dead, dehydrated" while the fort held 1,994 drinks. Later **occupied by the goblins of The Torments of Hating**, who evicted The Distant Letter. Its artifact The Nuanced Hamlet is said to still be there.
2. **Quakefocus** (~year 105-116). Founded starving: no drink, no meals, 17 plants for 10 dwarves ("famine, day 32"). Runaway work orders produced **1,981 armor stands and 148 thrones**. 16,000 smoothing jobs queued for 3 smoothers. Two of its four hospitals had no doctor. A fey carpenter died of thirst **beside 150 rough gems** (they were all needed elsewhere / cut). A possessed weaver died waiting for the loom room. A fey miner died of thirst while 7 silk scraps "hid" a cloth shortage. Holds the artifact **Claspedserpent** (a silver mace). Its paper was the Quakefocus Sentinel.
3. **Creaturemirrored** (now, year ~122; population ~147; the paper is the Creaturemirrored Pickaxe & Tribune). A miner died of thirst stuck **on a tree branch**. 15 dwarves died in the "Safety" burrow 30 levels below the fort when a forgotten beast came up the stairs. 8 dwarves were killed hauling refuse back out of the garbage pit while more was thrown in. Strange moods keep demanding ROUGH gems after every gem was cut (a fisherdwarf, a butcher, a weaver), and a **dwarven child** in a mood demanded plant cloth. A mist ring of pumps sprays water for happy thoughts; windmills; a cistern; catacombs; an execution tower. The militia is one captain and nine empty places, permanently "Off duty". The mayor's office keeps filing "production order violation" charges. Offices vacant: mayor, sheriff, hammerer, dungeon master, champion, chief medical dwarf, and more. A spectator camera tours rooms and follows guests (merchants, diplomats, elves, animal people) and jumps to threats.

## The wider world
- Our civilization: **The Chambers of Steel** (dwarves). Monarch: Oddom Oltartilesh. Outpost liaison: Iden Loloklolor (always says "There is much to share..." and recommends import agreements; once asked for rings at 210%). Diplomat: Ilral Rakustalnis. At peace with 16 groups; at war with 2 goblin groups: **The Destined Wickedness** (total war) and **The High Cruelties** (skirmishing).
- Goblin armies: **The Crazed Hex** (leaders Ngumrash Ensebrab, Laba Cócefevu Iwe) drove refugees out of Padtufted, Pulledrained and Ironcrowds; **The Vice of Fins** (Uslôx Ulutodan) also hit Padtufted. Refugee bands: The Plain Fellowship, The Society of Creeds, The Silks of Cremating, The Society of Adoration.
- Humans of **The Grooved Council** occupied Jackaldefense, evicting The Distinct Dungeon. Humans of The Fair Cakes live at Toeembrace.
- Dwarven groups reclaimed Tametomb (The Chaste Cudgel, leader Bomrek Thimshursåkzul) and are reclaiming Scaldedarch (The Oily Boots).
- Crooked horrors (giant beasts): **Omo Fewethauyira** near Shadyghosts, **Thone Nìnoana** at The Sable Nights.
- Elves abducted at Nobledessert (Mame Ayathemiyi) and Keeperglossed (Imara Sidayanithe).
- Successions: The Glowing Cult, The Wayward Doctrine, The Deified Lantern, The Distant Letter all changed leaders.
- The artifact **The Amusing Duties** (steel greaves) was lost at Roughnessslaps in year 68 and has been claimed by ~48 different figures; it sits at Fieryaged.

## Running jokes the stream has established
- Dwarves dying of thirst surrounded by drink/gems/materials (the "thirst paradox").
- The Overseer's runaway orders (1,981 armor stands, 148 thrones), smoothing everything, gathering plants across the whole map in the rain.
- "Safety" burrows that are not safe; the garbage pit; trees as death traps.
- Moods demanding the one thing the fort just ran out of; children having moods.
- The militia of one; offices nobody will take; the mayor's paperwork.
- Fort after fort in the same world; the previous forts' ghosts, ruins and goblin occupiers.
- The world occasionally stops and restarts ("the gods blinked"); crashes and reloads are 'the Great Rewinds' to the dwarves.
