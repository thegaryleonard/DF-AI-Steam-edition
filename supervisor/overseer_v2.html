The Fort's Future

Every fort ends. Retire this one while it stands (it stays in the world's history, and the Herald remembers it), then start again. You will be asked to type the fort's name to confirm.

A new fort in this world

Retires the fort, saves the world, embarks a few days' walk from here (river, no aquifer) and hands the new fort to df-ai. Same history, same neighbours, same grudges. About 15 minutes.

A new world

Retires the fort, generates a brand new world (the game's basic settings, as last used; generating history can take an hour or more), embarks in it and starts df-ai. The old world stays saved; the Herald starts its first issue for the new one.
